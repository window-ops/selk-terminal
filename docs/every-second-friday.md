Every second Friday is a story set in Glasgow after the Selk mission, in `notes/friday.html`. The developer notes page hides the way in: scrolling on past the end of `notes/devnotes.html` brings out a button that opens the story, and scrolling back up puts it away (`notes/js/friday-door.js`). The page is built from a story source by a script; `notes/js/friday.js` gives it the TABS and CONTENTS views, and `notes/css/friday.css` styles it.

## The story in brief

Cornelius, the supervisor of the Selk site on Titan, has lived in Glasgow for more than a year, and every second Friday he eats at the same restaurant counter in a shopping mall. A week after the news about Selk is published with his photograph, Kaylen, who works at the counter, stays past the end of her shift to talk to him.

Part 1, One Friday evening, follows the two of them over a few hours. At the table they talk about his twenty years on Titan, the filler and substance test, lojban, his old work forecasting debt crises, the Troika and the satirical games he made. On the way to the station they try the simulation of a CESEA museum exhibit on his phone, and they take the same train, where they talk about Scotland joining the Federation and the euro. On the train, Pavel Kratochvíl from GenPlan calls about a merge request for the unit package checker. Cornelius reviews it on his phone and finds a way to fill a unit's disk with quarantined updates, along with missing safety rules for forecasting units. He also tells Kaylen his real name, Cornel, and she keeps it to herself. They get off at her stop, walk through Unification Park reading CESEA incident reports, a GitHub repository and bug reports, and sit on a bench across from his building until he goes home.

Part 2, Her day off, takes place several days later, after the two have messaged each other in the evenings. On Kaylen's day off they meet at the bench in Unification Park with two cups of tea and talk about her double shifts, his four-hour working day and the GenPlan exams. Cornelius shows her NTorch 3, which assumes nothing: it answers a question about paint and a request to simulate Yugoslavia staying together with forms to fill in, and it finds the source of a sentence from a BBC documentary of 1979. Walking through the park, they talk about her clothes, anti-rejection education, the Dacian myths of the Romanian conservatives, the Scottish legends of Scota and the Stone of Destiny, her fear that people go away, and love. A presenter on a stranger's phone describes the discovery at Selk; the man recognises Cornelius, and Cornelius tells him what happened at the site. They watch a LiveOverflow video showing that the Selk incident was an insider attack and how the new package checker stops it, eat fish suppers at a chip shop near her station, and part at the path to his building, agreeing to meet again on Tuesday.

Part 3 has no chapters yet.

### A note for the reader

In this future, the LiveOverflow channel is kept going by a friend of its original author, a fictional person. Its videos therefore differ from the channel as it was in the 2010s and 2020s: the wording and the type of content have changed, a video has more chapters, and the chapter titles carry no "Chapter N".

## About this text

Every second Friday was written as a roleplay, one reply at a time. miculpionier set the scene, wrote the lines of Cornelius and supervised the text: each reply by Claude was read, and corrections were given as editing notes. Claude (Anthropic) wrote the lines of Kaylen, Pavel Kratochvíl, the presenter, the man on the bench and the man behind the counter, and the program screens. Every editing note was applied before the page was built, and the lines appear in the order they were written.
