Design

Selk is a database investigation game on the model of Lost wiki Kozlovka. The player is the only supervisor of a CESEA construction site on Titan, woken from a long sleep because the main tower is failing. The audit office on Earth sends report pages with blanks. The player fills each blank with the name of the database entry that answers it.

Two paths explain the failure. Path A follows the life-detection gate: an update restored a hydrogen baseline from 2083, the export plant had already lowered local hydrogen, and the gate froze repairs across zone 14. Path B follows the crack detector: the same update installed a model trained on Earth steel and concrete, and cutter units removed sound parts. Both paths lead to HX Holdings and a CESEA insider who holds release key 7.

Three kinds of unreliable text test the player. Model summaries contain errors. Some author labels are false, which the player can detect by comparing staff signatures with the dates of the long sleep, since the site has one crew member. Three handbook notes are outdated, which the edition year reveals.
